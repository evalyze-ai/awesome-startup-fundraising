This list collects tools, guides, programs, and data for raising a pre-seed to Series A round: evaluating your startup, improving pitch materials, finding investors, closing the round, and managing the whole process. It's maintained by the team at [Evalyze](https://www.evalyze.ai/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=awesome_list&utm_campaign=startup_fundraising_resources) and was last checked in October 2026.

## Investor Discovery & Matching

Platforms for finding, qualifying, and prioritising investors.

- [**Crunchbase**](https://www.crunchbase.com/) → Database of companies, investors, and funding rounds for market and investor research.

- [**Dealroom**](https://dealroom.co/) → Startup and VC database, widely used for ecosystem mapping (especially strong in Europe).

- [**Evalyze Investor Matching**](https://www.evalyze.ai/investor-matching?utm_source=github&utm_medium=awesome_list&utm_campaign=startup_fundraising_resources) → Matches founders with VCs and angels by stage, sector, location, check size, and investment history.

- [**OpenVC**](https://www.openvc.app/) → Free investor directory with filters for stage, geography, and thesis, plus an open submission format.
- [**VentureDex**](https://venturedex.co/) → Curated, source-linked startup research with funding signals and investor context.

- [**vcfirmsdirectory.com**](https://vcfirmsdirectory.com/) → Free-to-browse directory of VC firms in 65 countries, built from public registers and firm websites.

## CRM & Dealflow Management

CRMs and pipeline tools are often repurposed for tracking a fundraise.

- [**Affinity**](https://www.affinity.co/product/crm) → Relationship-intelligence CRM that auto-captures emails and meetings and helps track dealflow across the firm.

- [**Airtable**](https://www.airtable.com/) → Spreadsheet-database tool often used to build simple investor pipeline trackers.

- [**Attio**](https://attio.com/) → Flexible CRM with AI features, used by GTM and investing teams to model pipelines and workflows.

- [**Close**](https://close.com/) → Sales CRM with built-in calling, email, and sequences some founders use for investor outbound.

- [**Evalyze for Investors**](https://www.evalyze.ai/investors?utm_source=github&utm_medium=awesome_list&utm_campaign=startup_fundraising_resources) → AI due diligence and deal screening for VCs, with scoring, risk flags, and CRM and data room integrations.

- [**folk**](https://www.folk.app/) → Lightweight CRM for contacts and pipelines, with LinkedIn, Gmail, and Outlook integrations.

- [**HubSpot CRM**](https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm) → General-purpose CRM with contact, company, and pipeline tracking, popular with early-stage teams.

- [**Pipedrive**](https://www.pipedrive.com/) → Deal-centric CRM with visual pipelines that many founders reuse for investor outreach.

- [**Streak**](https://www.streak.com/) → Gmail-native CRM; manage fundraising pipelines directly from your inbox.

- [**Twenty**](https://twenty.com/) → Open-source CRM, self-hostable or cloud-hosted.

## Data Rooms & Document Management

Places to host decks, KPIs, and due diligence documents.

- [**Box**](https://www.box.com/) → Cloud file sharing with granular permissions and a free individual plan.

- [**Digify**](https://digify.com/) → Secure document sharing and virtual data rooms with tracking, watermarks, and access controls.

- [**DocSend**](https://www.docsend.com/) → Virtual data room and deck-sharing tool from Dropbox, with page-level analytics for fundraising and investor updates.

- [**Dropbox**](https://www.dropbox.com/) → File sync and sharing platform frequently used for larger diligence folders.

- [**Google Drive**](https://workspace.google.com/products/drive/) → General cloud storage and sharing; a common "good enough" data room for early rounds.

- [**Notion**](https://www.notion.com/) → All-in-one workspace for docs and databases; often used to host lightweight data rooms and FAQs.

- [**Papermark**](https://www.papermark.com/) → Open-source virtual data room and secure document sharing with analytics and custom domains.

- [**Peony**](https://www.peony.ink/) → Virtual data room with per-document permissions and page-level engagement analytics.

## Legal Docs, SAFEs & Incorporation

Standard documents and services for forming a company and closing early rounds.

- [**Clerky**](https://www.clerky.com/) → Online startup legal paperwork: incorporation, SAFEs, and hiring documents.

- [**Firstbase**](https://www.firstbase.io/) → US company formation for founders anywhere, plus registered agent, mailroom, and accounting add-ons.

- [**NVCA Model Legal Documents**](https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/) → Free model term sheet and Series A financing documents from the National Venture Capital Association.

- [**SeedLegals**](https://seedlegals.com/) → UK legal and fundraising platform for SEIS/EIS assurance, share issues, and round paperwork.

- [**Stripe Atlas**](https://stripe.com/atlas) → Online Delaware C corp or LLC formation, with stock issuance, 83(b) filing, and SAFE generation.

- [**YC SAFE Documents**](https://www.ycombinator.com/safe) → Y Combinator's free post-money SAFE forms and user guide, including Canada, Cayman, and Singapore versions.

## Accelerators & Programs

Programs that invest in very early teams and help them raise their first rounds.

- [**a16z speedrun**](https://speedrun.a16z.com/) → Andreessen Horowitz's twelve-week San Francisco program for early-stage startups.

- [**Antler**](https://www.antler.co/) → Global pre-seed investor running founder residencies in many cities.

- [**Creative Destruction Lab**](https://creativedestructionlab.com/) → Nonprofit, objectives-based program for seed-stage science and technology startups, founded at the University of Toronto.

- [**Entrepreneurs First**](https://www.joinef.com/) → Talent investor that backs individuals before they have a cofounder or an idea.

- [**Founder Institute**](https://fi.co/) → Part-time pre-seed accelerator with chapters worldwide; founders pay an entrance fee.

- [**Techstars**](https://www.techstars.com/accelerators) → Network of three-month, mentorship-driven accelerator programs worldwide.

- [**Y Combinator**](https://www.ycombinator.com/apply) → Three-month San Francisco accelerator with a standard $500K investment, running four batches a year.

## Non-Dilutive Funding

Grants, tax credits, and government programs that fund startups without taking equity.

- [**EIC Accelerator (EU)**](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en) → EU grant of up to €2.5M plus optional equity investment for innovative startups and SMEs.

- [**Innovate UK (UK)**](https://www.ukri.org/councils/innovate-uk/) → UK innovation agency offering competitive grants and other support for business R&D.

- [**NRC IRAP (Canada)**](https://nrc.canada.ca/en/support-technology-innovation) → Advisory services and R&D funding for Canadian small and medium-sized businesses.

- [**NSF America's Seed Fund (US)**](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/) → National Science Foundation SBIR/STTR program funding R&D at startups and small businesses.

- [**SBIR / STTR (US)**](https://www.sbir.gov/) → US federal R&D grants and contracts for small businesses across participating agencies.

- [**SR&ED Tax Incentive (Canada)**](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/scientific-research-experimental-development-tax-incentive-program.html) → Canadian tax deductions and investment tax credits for eligible R&D work.
